Our Story

More than fitness.
It's intentional.

Ethereal Balance began with a belief that wellness is a bridge between people, and grew into a community moving together. Based in Southern California, with experiences nationwide.

Est.2024Building Community
The Founders

A vision, rooted in intention.

Ethereal Balance was founded by Jazmin Sabah with a deeply personal mission — to create spaces where wellness becomes a way to bring people together through mindful movement.

What began in 2024 as intimate gatherings has grown into partnerships with leading hotels and companies across San Diego and Southern California. Megan Pappa, Director of Operations, keeps the ship steady — this vision simply wouldn't happen without her.

Every event is designed with intention. Every movement has purpose. Every connection matters.

The EB Method
Our Method

Trained in the EB Method.

Beyond our own gatherings, we teach a board-based methodology and certify independent instructors to carry it into their own practice. It is hands-on, built around time under tension and intentional movement, and designed so every level is supported in the same room.
